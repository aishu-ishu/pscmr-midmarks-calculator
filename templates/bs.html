Guide: How to Use This Site Correctly

🎉 You are lucky enough for the site to open right now! This application only allows a maximum of 2 concurrent users at a time. If more than 2 users try to use it simultaneously, the site will get stuck on loading.

1. Uploading the Right Image Format & Spacing

To make sure the extractor doesn't miss columns, your image must fulfill these conditions:

  • Avoid Cropping: Do not cut off spaces on edges of the image.
  • Clear Text & High Resolution: Blurry or heavily compressed phone camera photos can fail character recognition. Clear screenshots work best.

Visual Reference: Correct Margins & Spacing

Notice how there is a clear boundary/blank space around the table edges try to maintain it while uploading.

⚠️ Important: Leave some empty margin space around the table when taking a screenshot. If the image is cropped without spaces, the parser will fail to read it and misses some data!

2. Processing Expectations

  • Image extraction and calculations typically take around 1 to 3 minutes to complete. Please be patient and do not refresh while it loads.
  • For accurate predictions, upload sheets containing marks for both mid-term exams.
